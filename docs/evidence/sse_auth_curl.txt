Standard browser EventSource cannot send custom Authorization headers. Instead of exposing
bearer tokens in URL query strings (where they are logged in proxy access logs and browser history),
the Cerina frontend uses '@microsoft/fetch-event-source', a fetch-based SSE client that dispatches
